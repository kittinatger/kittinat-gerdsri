Magnolia

Eco-cultural resort, reimagined

Magnolia is a fictional eco-cultural resort concept set on 18 acres near Lake Conlin, just outside St. Cloud, Florida — a fully coded, responsive marketing site exploring hospitality branding, information architecture, and UI design for a retreat with four distinct "wings," each inspired by a different region of Asia, surrounding a shared garden atrium.

View Live Site → View on GitHub →

What it does

Four wings, one garden

Four regional buildings — South, East, North, and West — each with its own architectural story drawn from a different corner of Asia, arranged around a shared Flower Garden Atrium at the property's center. Every wing is clickable for detail, matching the site's shared popup/modal system used across every interactive section.

Rooms & the grounds

Four room tiers — Standard, Luxury Suites, Family Room Suite, and Resort Villas & Bungalows — each with a demo reservation form, alongside the grounds themselves: a restaurant, spa, art gallery, pool/sauna/onsen, and market, each detailed in a shared popup.

Experiences & events

Year-round programming across all four seasons, on-the-water and on-the-trail activities, cool-season programming on the lake, weddings and private events, a self-updating calendar of weekend shows and gatherings, and two membership tiers — Bud & Bloom.

Sustainability

Rooftop solar across all four wings, rainwater collection and low-flow fixtures, native and drought-tolerant landscaping, and regionally sourced restaurant and market vendors — sustainability was the starting point for how Magnolia is built and run, not an afterthought layered onto how it looks.

The business concept

The site is grounded in a full business model canvas covering key partners, activities, and resources, the value proposition, customer segments, channels, cost structure, and revenue streams — the kind of strategic thinking that sits underneath the branding and UI, not just a marketing site designed in isolation.

Why I built it

Most of my coding work is app-shaped — Tally is a good example. Magnolia was a deliberate change of medium: a chance to practice hospitality branding and information architecture end to end, from a business model canvas through visual identity to a fully responsive, interactive site — without the constraints of a real client or a real property. It sits at the intersection of design and code on purpose, which is why it belongs in both places on this site.

How it works

Magnolia is a static single-page site — plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, no framework or build step — deployed via GitHub Pages directly from the repository. A consistent popup/modal system handles every clickable section (the four wings, room tiers, grounds, activities), and the whole layout is fully responsive from mobile through desktop. Typography pairs Fraunces (serif) for display headings with Jost (sans-serif) for body text, both via Google Fonts, carrying the brand's calm, editorial feel through every section.

Try it yourself

The concept site is live and free to browse — explore the four wings, the room tiers, and the grounds, or open the reservation form to see the demo booking flow. Nothing is bookable; it's a design and development exercise, not a real hotel.
