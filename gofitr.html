GoFitr

Fitness tracking, kept private

GoFitr is a local-first fitness tracker: log workouts with sets, reps, and weight, track body weight over time, and log meals against a fully local database of 500+ foods — all without an account or a server, since everything is stored right in your browser. Sign in with Google, Apple, or GitHub if you want a portable login, or just create a local username and password; either way, your data never leaves your device.

Try GoFitr → View on GitHub →

What it does

Workout tracking

Log workouts with multiple exercises, each with its own sets, reps, weight, and notes. A dashboard surfaces your current streak, weekly training volume, and weekly and total workout counts, and full workout history is filterable per exercise. Progress charts track max weight and total volume over time, alongside automatically tracked personal records.

Nutrition tracking

A daily food log against calorie and macro goals, backed by a built-in database of 500+ common foods, drinks, desserts, and dishes — browsable by category or searchable, with no external food API involved. Custom foods, saved meals, and bookmarked foods make repeat logging fast, and picking any food opens a detail view with serving size, macros, and a full micronutrient breakdown before it's added.

Body weight & accounts

Body weight logging with a trend chart, a kg/lb unit toggle throughout, and full export/import of your data as JSON, or clearing it entirely. Sign in with Google, Apple, or GitHub through Clerk, or create a local account — each account's data stays private on that device, since nothing is synced to a server.

Why I built it

Most fitness and nutrition apps want an account, a subscription, and a copy of your data on their servers before they'll let you log a single set. GoFitr is the opposite bet: everything — workouts, meals, body weight, even the food database — lives entirely in your own browser, with social login available for convenience but never required. It's also a chance to build something substantial without reaching for a framework: no build step, no bundler, just HTML, CSS, and JavaScript doing the work directly.

How it works

GoFitr is vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with no build step, using Chart.js for the progress and trend charts and Clerk for optional Google, Apple, and GitHub sign-in, run entirely from the client with no server-side auth code involved. All workout, nutrition, and body weight data lives in the browser's localStorage, scoped per account so local accounts on the same device stay isolated from each other. The nutrition database's 500+ entries, including full micronutrient breakdowns, are hand-authored and served locally rather than pulled from a live food API.

Try it yourself

The live app is free to use — create a local account in seconds, or sign in with Google, Apple, or GitHub, and start logging a workout or a meal. Nothing is sent anywhere but your own browser's storage.

Good to know

GoFitr is still actively in development — features, design, and stored data are all subject to change without notice, so don't rely on it yet for anything you can't afford to lose. Since data lives in localStorage rather than a server, it's tied to the specific browser and device you used to log it: clearing site data, switching browsers, or switching devices means starting over unless you've exported a backup. The built-in nutrition database's values are hand-authored approximations from general nutrition knowledge, not sourced from a lab or certified database.
