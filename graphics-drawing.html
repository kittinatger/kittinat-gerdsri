Graphics / Drawing

Inspired by emotion

Art and design are my way of exploring how imagination transforms into visual expression. I'm driven by the challenge of turning ideas into forms that communicate, inspire, and connect — while refining my own creativity and sense of detail along the way. From crafting sleek digital interfaces to illustrating dynamic, expressive scenes, I focus on blending emotion with visual precision. I stay curious, inventive, and committed to creating visuals that feel alive and meaningful.

KAO Environment Painting Contest

Let's Use Bioplastics - A factory rendered as a faint, almost invisible outline stands at the top of a thriving valley, its everyday packaging built not from petroleum but from the crops growing right below it — corn, sugarcane, and coconut rise up the hillside to become a cup, a box, and a bag, materials that return to the earth instead of piling up in it. Because the factory leaves such a light footprint, the valley around it stays alive: terraced cornfields ripple down the slopes, sugarcane grows tall and green, and a family camps, fishes, and farms along the clean river below. - 13th KAO International Environment Painting Contest 2022 - Hand-drawn

Toyota Dream Car Art Contest

RBTW Machine - Short for "Rebuild The World," this solar-powered vehicle rolls in on a conveyor belt built for one job: sorting the mess left behind. Trash rides past it in a line and drops into color-coded bins — hazardous waste, recyclables, general garbage, and food scraps each sent their own way, with a composting chamber turning what's left into fresh soil for a garden growing right along the machine's back. Up front, two tall tanks pull water in and pump it through a hose that snakes off toward the wildfire raging on the horizon, dousing the flames while a rabbit rides shotgun on top. On the left, the world it's rebuilding: a clean river, a waterfall, and a hillside crowded with elephants, a tiger, a lion, giraffes, deer, and a swan — proof of what's still there to save on the other side of the smoke. - TOYOTA Dream Car Art Contest 2022 - Adobe Illustrator
